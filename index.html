Someone who read it all first

The powerful have always had an assistant who reads everything first and tells them what matters. Now everyone gets one, built to the same standard.

One shared newsroom reads the day’s news once and checks every claim against its source. Then each app makes it for one person. DayStart reads it to you. Plain Ballot covers your ballot. Artifact News draws it.
