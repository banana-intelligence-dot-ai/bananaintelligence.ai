## What is DayStart?

The free edition is one show a day, the same for everyone, about 4 minutes long. No account, no card. Open the iPhone app and press play while you get ready.

Every show leads with the day’s single Top Story. When the story turns on something someone said, the quote stays in their own words, attributed to who said it. The news comes from a mix of sources.

## How does the morning show play?

It plays as audio with AI voices, and every story shows on screen as a card that follows along. Swipe to the next story, or just listen. It keeps playing in the background and from the lock screen.

You can set a morning reminder at the time you pick, and one tap plays the show. Or start it with your alarm: after a guided setup, stop your alarm and your DayStart starts. You can also say “Hey Siri, play my DayStart.”

## What does Premium add?

Premium builds the show around your day: your calendar, the weather where you are, and the news, markets and sports you pick. A lighter edition runs on weekends.

Premium comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. DayStart is free to use.

## Who it’s for

Anyone who wants the morning news in their ears while they get ready. Look up and follow along in story cards while it plays.
