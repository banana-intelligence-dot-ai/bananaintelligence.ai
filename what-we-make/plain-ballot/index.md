## What it is

Plain Ballot is a free, nonpartisan, open-source guide to your ballot. Type your address and see where each candidate stands, quoted exactly, with a link to the source.

Party labels stay hidden until you ask, and it never says how to vote.

## Who it’s for

Any voter who wants to know who’s on the ballot and what each candidate said or did. Assistants can use it too, through its read-only MCP server.
