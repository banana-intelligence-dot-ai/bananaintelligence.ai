## What it is

Spooky Particles watches the apps you ship on Vercel. When one quietly stops, your coding agent gets the evidence and your phone gets an alert. It checks every live site every 5 minutes.

It watches deploys, pages and scheduled jobs, and helps you automate the fixes.

## Who it’s for

People who ship apps on Vercel with their own coding agent, like Claude Code or Codex, and want to know when a deploy, a page or a scheduled job breaks.
