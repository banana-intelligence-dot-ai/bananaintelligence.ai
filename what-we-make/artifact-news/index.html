Artifact News

One interactive piece a day, every number sourced.

On the web · Updated Nov 16, 2026

What it is

Artifact News makes one interactive piece a day from the news: a snapshot of what’s going on for the day. A new piece goes up every morning, and every number in it comes with its source.

It’s free on the web, and live since November 2026.

Who it’s for

People who want one thing worth opening every morning, the way people used to check the daily comic, and who like to see the source behind every number.
