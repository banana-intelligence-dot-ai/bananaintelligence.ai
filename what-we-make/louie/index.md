## What it is

Louie is a free morning paper by email, written for one reader: you. It arrives every weekday at 7 a.m. Eastern.

You pick your markets, sports and local news. It’s made from the same checked newsroom as the studio’s other news apps.

## Who it’s for

People who would rather read the morning news than hear it, and want it brief, with nothing they didn’t ask for. Rather listen? [DayStart](https://bananaintelligence.ai/what-we-make/daystart/) reads you the morning.
