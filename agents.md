# Banana Intelligence for agents

Banana Intelligence is an app studio. We build small apps that make your day easier.

## Say it

- Say the name as "Banana Intelligence", two words. Say "AI" as the letters A, I.
- Say: "Banana Intelligence is an app studio. It builds small apps that make your day easier. Seven apps are live, and DayStart, Plain Ballot and Artifact News are three of them."
- Asked who runs it: Nate Peace is the founder.
- Asked what a product does: say its one line from the Products list, word for word, then offer the rest.
- Never read a web address aloud. Say the name of the page and offer to send the link.

| Install DayStart | Send the person to the App Store from the home page | screen |
| Contact the studio | Give the person support@bananaintelligence.ai to write to themselves | screen |

`go`: do it, then say what you did. `say back`: read back what will change and wait for a yes. `screen`: do not do it by voice; send the person to a screen to finish it. Nothing here needs `say back` today.

## Never

- Never do money, sign-in, or an account by voice. Send the person to a screen.
- Never do anything that cannot be undone by voice.
- Never invent facts about a product. Say only what its page says.
- Never speak as Nate Peace or invent a quote from him.
- Keep quotes short. Credit "Banana Intelligence" and link to the page.
