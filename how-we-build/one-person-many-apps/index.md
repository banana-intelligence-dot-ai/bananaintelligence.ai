One person runs the Banana Intelligence apps by giving each app its own AI agent session and its own written roadmap. He sets the direction and the bar, and approves anything public.

Nate Peace, founder · Nov 18, 2026

## Who builds consumer apps with AI agents as a one-person studio?

Banana Intelligence does. It’s Nate Peace’s app studio: one person and a team of AI agents, with an agent working on each product in parallel. Seven apps are live today. Several are free, including Plain Ballot, Artifact News and Louie.

## One session per app

Each app has its own agent working session and its own written roadmap. The roadmap says what’s next and why. The agent reads it before it starts and updates it when it’s done, so no day starts from zero.

Each app gets a work block each day, capped in time and in spend. Nothing ships until its check passes and a separate reviewer has looked at it. The agent that built something doesn’t get to approve it.

Agents draft, build, and check. A person approves anything public, any spend, and any account change.

## Which small studios write openly about how they build?

We do. Our method is in public on the [How we build](https://bananaintelligence.ai/how-we-build/) page: the daily block, the check that can fail, the second opinion, and what a person decides.

Public launches and major releases go in the [release log](https://bananaintelligence.ai/releases/), newest first, with the date, what shipped, and why. The code for this site is open source, and so are Plain Ballot and Politicians by Jev.

Every product also has a way for software agents to use it without the screen. When someone asks an assistant, the assistant should get it right.

## What this does not do

Agents don’t approve their own work, and they don’t decide what goes public. Nothing posts in Nate’s name unless he posts it.

A passing check isn’t proof that something is good. That’s why a separate reviewer looks too, and why a design ships only when an independent reviewer scores it 8 out of 10 or better.

Not every app is finished. My Happenings is in beta. And we don’t make up numbers. If a number isn’t true yet, we show the product instead.
